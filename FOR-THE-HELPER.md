# Putting the live stream up — a one-page guide

*Hand this to whoever is helping on 9 August. It takes about two minutes.*

> **This guide also lives inside the site** at **yourdomain.com/admin/help** — there's a
> **Guide** button in the dashboard header and an **Open the guide** button on the Live
> stream tab. That version has a Print button too. Sending them the link is easier than
> sending this file.

## What you're doing

Akshay and Shraddha's wedding invitation has a spot for a live video. It's empty until someone pastes the link in. That's you.

## Step 1 — Start the stream

On a well-charged phone, on a tripod, pointed at the mandap:

1. Open the **YouTube** app → **+** → **Go live**
2. Title it something like *Akshay & Shraddha — Akshata Muhurat*
3. Set visibility to **Unlisted** (not Public, not Private)
4. Start it a few minutes before **12:00 PM**
5. Tap **Share** and copy the link

> Don't use Private — Unlisted means anyone with the invitation can watch, but it won't show up in YouTube search.

## Step 2 — Paste it into the invitation

1. Go to **yourdomain.com/admin**
2. Log in with the username and password Akshay gave you
3. You'll land on the **Live stream** tab
4. Paste the link into the box

The page tells you straight away what will happen — you should see **"YouTube — plays embedded inside the invitation"** in green, and a small preview of the video appears.

5. Optionally type a note like *"Starts at 11:55"*
6. Press **Save link**

That's it. Within a minute, everyone with the invitation open sees the video — including people who opened it hours ago.

## If something looks wrong

**Red text saying it doesn't look like a link** — you've probably pasted something that isn't a URL. It needs to start with `https://`.

**It says "Google Meet — shows a Join button"** — that's fine, it just means you pasted the Meet link instead of YouTube. Meet can't play inside a web page (Google blocks it), so guests get a button that opens the Meet app. Works, just not as smooth.

**No preview appears** — the link may be a Private stream. Set it to Unlisted in YouTube.

**You need to take it down** — press **Clear**, then **Save link**. The video section disappears.

## While you're in there

The other tabs are worth a glance if anyone asks:

- **RSVPs** — who's coming, and the meal counts for the kitchen
- **Akshata** — people watching from far away can throw rice on their phones at 12:00; this shows who did
- **Blessings** — messages guests have left. If anything inappropriate appears, press **Hide**.
